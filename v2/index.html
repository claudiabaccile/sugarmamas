Good to know

How far ahead should I order?

We need at least 14 days of lead time. For custom orders, reach out about a month before your event.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. Custom orders need a 50% deposit no less than 2 weeks before the event date.

Do you have gluten-friendly options?

Our macarons are gluten-friendly and available every day in the storefront.

Can I buy something the same day?

Stop by during open hours for cupcakes, cookies and macarons from the case. Custom work always needs advance notice.
