Real People, Real Bakers

Best friends,
baking since day one.

Yanett Ortiz and Debbie Andrade are best friends and the proud owners of Sugar Mama's Cakes & Bakery, located in La Habra, California since 2022.

Their shared interests and talent for baking brought them closer together when they met while working in the bakery at the Disneyland Resort.

Their cake art and hyper-realistic baked goods have been featured on notable television contests such as Food Network's Winner Cake All and Cakealikes.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Fill out our quote form and our team will follow up with a custom quote within 2 business days.

Where can I pick up my order?

All orders are picked up at our bakery: 2408 W Whittier Blvd, La Habra, CA 90631.

What are your hours?

Tue – Wed & Friday, 10AM – 6PM · Saturday, 10AM – 4PM · Closed Sunday, Monday & Thursday.

Do you accommodate allergies?

Please let us know about nut allergies when ordering — some of our flavors and fillings contain almonds, peanuts or pecans, and our macarons are made with almond flour.

What occasions do you cater to?

Weddings, birthdays, baby showers, quinceañeras, corporate events, and any "just because" moment worth celebrating.
