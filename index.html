Meet the mamas

Best friends,
baking since day one.

Yanett Ortiz and Debbie Andrade are best friends and the proud owners of Sugar Mamas Cakes & Bakery, located in La Habra, California since 2022.

Their shared interests and talent for baking brought them closer together when they met while working in the bakery at the Disneyland Resort.

Their cake art and hyper-realistic baked goods have been featured on notable television contests such as Food Network’s Winner Cake All and Cakealikes.

Yanett and Debbie’s team are passionate and dedicated bakers who strive to get every detail of your order just right. Get in touch with a Sugar Mamas Bakery representative to plan your event’s desserts today!
